The Constitutional Personality Map

Every national constitution, scored on four personality axes, clustered into archetypes and given a Myers–Briggs type. Pick a lens, then find your country.

Dot colour is each country’s own type: warm = E, cool = I; vivid = N, muted = S; dark = J, light = P. Tap a type to isolate it.

Archetypes

Clusters of constitutions that scored alike. Select one to light it up on the map.

    What the four letters mean in this lens

    Method. Claude read each constitution and scored it from −1 to +1 on four axes per lens. Constitutions with similar scores sit close together on the map (UMAP); archetypes are the clusters on that map (HDBSCAN), named by Claude. Treat it as a parlour game with footnotes.

    Built by Simon Podhajský & Claude. Inspired by Gwyneth Windflower asking “what if constitutions had MBTI types?” Texts from the Constitute Project.
